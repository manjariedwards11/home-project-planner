Phase 2 — Existing Planter Feasibility

Existing planter: 11 ft × 3 ft × 22 in, already lined and visually matched to the wall.

Full geometric volume is roughly 450 gallons. Actual operating volume would be lower. Several hundred gallons can still mean 3,000+ lb of water plus continuous lateral pressure.

Before removing soil, verify:
  • Framing lumber dimensions and spacing
  • Cross-bracing
  • Plywood/sheathing
  • Bottom support and anchoring
  • Liner material, thickness, seams and condition
  • Patio/slab suitability

Phase 3 — Pump + Filter + Fountain

POND ↓ PUMP — continuously moves water ↓ MECHANICAL FILTER — dirt / leaves / dead algae ↓ BIOLOGICAL FILTER — beneficial bacteria ↓ SIMPLE FOUNTAIN / RETURN — movement + oxygen ↓ POND OPTIONAL LATER: UV clarifier for persistent green-water algae
Existing $30 smartpond kit: keep it for the small nursery. Do not size the permanent pond around it.
