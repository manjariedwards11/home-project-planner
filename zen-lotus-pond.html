Phase 1 — Lotus Nursery

Status: complete.

  • Three seed-grown lotus seedlings
  • Aquatic planting media
  • Solid planting pot
  • White gravel cap
  • Conditioned water
  • Small circulation/fountain

Some nursery algae is normal. Remove visible debris/string algae instead of repeatedly restarting the whole setup.

Phase 2 — Pond Decision

Existing planter

  • 11 ft × 3 ft × 22 in
  • Already lined
  • Already matches the wall
  • Could approach ~450 gallons geometrically if completely filled

Before removing soil

  • Verify framing size and spacing
  • Check cross-bracing and sheathing
  • Check bottom support and anchoring
  • Identify liner type and thickness
  • Inspect seams and penetrations
  • Confirm patio/slab support
Why: several hundred gallons can create 3,000+ lb of water plus continuous lateral pressure.

Phase 3 — Pump + Filter + Fountain

POND → PUMP → MECHANICAL FILTER → BIOLOGICAL FILTER → SIMPLE FOUNTAIN / RETURN → POND OPTIONAL LATER: UV clarifier only if persistent green-water algae develops
$30 smartpond kit: keep it for the nursery. Do not size the permanent pond around it.

Phase 4 — Structure / Liner

If the existing planter is sufficiently reinforced and its liner is pond-grade and intact, this phase may be minimal. Otherwise reinforce the structure and install pond-grade underlayment/liner before filling.

Phase 5 — Jasmine

Layout: Jasmine — Pond — Jasmine.

  • Two white textured pots
  • Two black trellises
  • Light jasmine density
  • Reliable watering for extreme summer heat

Phase 6 — Lotus + Buddha

  • Lotus occupies one end/corner
  • Buddha sits beside the lotus
  • Simple fountain sits toward the opposite side
  • Preserve open reflective water
  • Start fishless

Phase 7 — Pothos

Use pothos in the white wall pots only where protected from intense direct summer sun. Keep the planting sparse and natural.

Phase 8 — Lighting

  • Warm jasmine uplights
  • Subtle Buddha light
  • Restrained pond-edge lighting
  • Primary filtration uses reliable continuous power
