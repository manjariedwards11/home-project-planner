Phase 1 — Lotus Nursery

Status: complete.

  • Three seed-grown lotus seedlings
  • Aquatic planting media
  • Solid planting pot
  • White gravel cap
  • Conditioned water
  • Small circulation/fountain

Some nursery algae is normal. Remove visible debris/string algae instead of repeatedly restarting the whole setup.

Phase 2 — Pond Decision

Existing planter

  • 11 ft × 3 ft × 22 in
  • Already lined
  • Already matches the wall
  • Could approach ~450 gallons geometrically if completely filled

Before removing soil

  • Verify framing size and spacing
  • Check cross-bracing
  • Check plywood/sheathing
  • Check bottom support and anchoring
  • Identify liner material/thickness
  • Inspect seams and penetrations
  • Confirm patio/slab support
Why: several hundred gallons can create 3,000+ lb of water plus continuous lateral pressure against the 11-foot walls.

Phase 3 — Pump + Filter + Fountain

POND ↓ PUMP ↓ MECHANICAL FILTER — catches dirt, leaves and dead algae ↓ BIOLOGICAL FILTER — supports beneficial bacteria ↓ SIMPLE FOUNTAIN / RETURN — surface movement + oxygen ↓ POND OPTIONAL LATER: UV clarifier if persistent green-water algae develops
$30 smartpond kit: keep it for the small nursery. Do not size the permanent pond around it.

Phase 4 — Structure / Liner

This phase may be very small or disappear. If the existing planter is sufficiently reinforced and its liner is pond-grade and intact, most of the work is already done.

If not, reinforce the structure and install pond-grade underlayment/liner before filling.

Phase 5 — Jasmine

Layout: Jasmine — Pond — Jasmine, all in the same row.

  • Two selected white textured pots
  • Two black trellises
  • Light jasmine density, not a solid wall of foliage
  • Large soil volume and reliable watering for 110°F summer heat

Phase 6 — Lotus + Buddha

  • Lotus occupies one end/corner
  • Buddha sits beside the lotus
  • Simple fountain sits toward the opposite side
  • Preserve a large central area of open reflective water
  • Start fishless; fish are not required

Phase 7 — Pothos

Pothos can trail from the white wall pots only where protected from intense direct summer sun. It is not a 110°F full-sun plant.

Use fewer plants and let them trail naturally instead of filling every pot densely.

Phase 8 — Lighting

  • Warm solar jasmine uplights
  • Subtle Buddha light
  • Restrained pond-edge lighting
  • Primary filtration should use reliable continuous power
